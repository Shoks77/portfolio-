About 自己紹介

Hi, I'm Shoki. I'm from Tokyo, with a perspective shaped by studying in Washington, D.C., and working in New York.

Curious about what makes people love a product — and what makes the business behind it work. I enjoy connecting creative ideas with consumer insights and numbers, whether exploring a product concept, shaping a brand story, or bringing communities together.

I recently completed a brand strategy marketing internship with L'Oréal Paris in New York. Before that, experiences in Michelin-starred dining and at The Ritz-Carlton Tokyo taught me to notice the small things: what people care about, what they need, and what they don't always say.

Now, back in Tokyo, I'm exploring new ideas and connecting passionate communities through Figma and etc.

Always happy to meet ventures and start-up people building something they care about for international benefits, in Japanese or English.
